Privacy

AI Engineering Arena · last updated 24 November 2026

The AI Engineering Arena is a free learning game run by two friends, TWadi and GhassenJamoussi99 (Carthavate). It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. This page says what we store, where, and how to have it deleted. Questions: carthavateai@gmail.com.

Without signing in

You can read the lessons and watch the leaderboard without an account. We store nothing about you.

When you sign in

You sign in with GitHub or Google. Our database (Supabase, hosted in the EU, Frankfurt) then stores:

  • Your sign-in: your account ID and email address from GitHub or Google. We use them only to sign you in and never show your email on the site.
  • With GitHub: your GitHub username, public name and avatar, which become your public player profile.
  • With Google: the name Google sends us. Only the two admins see it, when you ask to join. Your public profile shows only the player name you pick, never your Google name or photo.

When you play

Once an admin lets you in, your player name, avatar, progress, quiz scores, duels, races and solved challenges appear on the public leaderboard and your profile. In a race, the other player sees the code you submitted.

What stays on your device

  • Python and the AI models run in your browser. What you type into them isn't sent to us.
  • Your notes for "Ask the course" are kept in your browser's storage and never uploaded.
  • In the Arena app on Anna (anna.partners), your notes are kept in the app's private storage in your Anna account, and questions to "Ask the course" are answered by Anna's AI. Anna's own privacy policy covers that.

Other services the site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site, and GitHub or Google signs you in.
  • Supabase stores the data above.
  • Hugging Face and jsDelivr serve the AI models and Python the first time you use them.
  • Lesson videos play from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).

These services see your IP address when your browser contacts them, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Email carthavateai@gmail.com and we'll delete your account. That erases your sign-in, your profile and everything you did here, including the duels you played. Your browser notes you can delete yourself in the app, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.
